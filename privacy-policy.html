Privacy Policy for Nonogram Visual Novel

Last updated: September 29, 2026

Nonogram Visual Novel is a puzzle game provided by Three Park. This policy explains how information is handled when you use the game.

Information we collect

The game does not require an account and does not ask for your name, email address, contacts, precise location, photos, or payment information.

We use Google AdMob to show optional rewarded ads. AdMob and its advertising partners may collect device identifiers, approximate location inferred from an IP address, app interaction data, and diagnostic information to deliver, measure, and improve ads. Ad personalization choices are managed through the consent and privacy options presented in the game where required by law.

We use Firebase Analytics to understand game usage and improve the game. This may include device and app information, diagnostic information, gameplay events (such as level starts and completions), and in-game progress values. We do not use Firebase Analytics to identify you by name.

How information is used

Information is used to operate the game, provide and measure optional rewarded advertising, analyze game performance and gameplay, prevent abuse, and improve the game.

Sharing

Information is processed by Google services used by the game, including Google AdMob and Firebase Analytics, subject to their applicable privacy policies. We do not sell personal information directly.

Data retention and security

Analytics and advertising data are retained by the relevant service providers according to their policies and settings. We use service providers with reasonable technical and organizational safeguards, but no transmission or storage system is completely secure.

Children

The game is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children.

Your choices

You can decline or change advertising consent where the game presents those choices. You can also reset or limit your advertising identifier through your device settings, subject to your device and region.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact koreash12@gmail.com.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the game or applicable requirements change. The updated version will be posted at this URL with a revised date.
